Question 5 of 6 · Using the OCI Generative AI Agents Service
A Generative AI Agent's knowledge base references a bucket in OCI Object Storage. When the agent attempts to ingest the documents, it fails with an authorization error. What is the MOST likely missing configuration?
The Agents service authenticates via a resource principal; a dynamic group matching that principal must be granted an IAM policy allowing read access to the bucket for ingestion to succeed.
