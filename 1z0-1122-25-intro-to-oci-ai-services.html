Question 1 of 6 · Intro to OCI AI Services
A logistics company receives thousands of scanned bills of lading and needs to automatically extract key-value pairs such as shipper name, consignee, and shipment date, plus table line items, without first labeling training data. Which OCI AI service BEST meets this requirement?
OCI Document Understanding is purpose-built to extract text, tables, and key-value pairs from scanned documents like invoices and bills of lading using pretrained models, requiring no custom training.
Question 2 of 6 · Intro to OCI AI Services
A call center wants to transcribe recorded customer service calls and needs the transcript to indicate which speaker (agent or customer) said each sentence. Which OCI AI Service capability enables this?
OCI Speech converts audio to text and supports speaker diarization, which labels transcript segments by speaker, exactly the capability needed here.
Question 3 of 6 · Intro to OCI AI Services
Which underlying algorithm does OCI Anomaly Detection use to identify anomalies across multiple correlated sensor signals simultaneously in operational data?
OCI Anomaly Detection is built on the Multivariate State Estimation Technique (MSET2), which models correlations between multiple signals to detect subtle anomalies earlier than univariate threshold methods.
Question 4 of 6 · Intro to OCI AI Services
A utility company wants to predict next month's electricity demand using three years of historical hourly consumption data. Which OCI AI service is purpose-built for this task?
OCI Forecasting is designed specifically to generate future predictions from historical time-series operational data, such as demand forecasting from consumption history.
Question 5 of 6 · Intro to OCI AI Services
A developer wants to integrate OCI Language sentiment analysis directly into a Python application without manually using the OCI Console. Which approach is appropriate?
OCI AI Services, including Language, expose REST APIs and are also accessible via language SDKs (including Python), allowing direct programmatic integration into applications.
Question 6 of 6 · Intro to OCI AI Services
A healthcare company needs to automatically redact patient names, phone numbers, and social security numbers from free-text clinical notes before sharing the data with a research partner. Which OCI AI service capability directly supports this requirement?
OCI Language includes a pretrained PII detection capability that identifies and can mask sensitive entities such as names, phone numbers, and SSNs within free text.
