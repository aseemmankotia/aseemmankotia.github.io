Question 1 of 6 · Client Engagement, Consulting, and Demo Delivery
You're three minutes into a live demo to the client's C-suite when the RAG system returns a visibly hallucinated output on a slide the CFO is scrutinizing closely. What is the BEST immediate action?
FDE demo craft under uncertainty requires a rehearsed failover plan: briefly acknowledge the glitch to maintain credibility, then pivot to a pre-validated backup asset so the narrative momentum and executive attention are preserved without dwelling on the failure.
Question 2 of 6 · Client Engagement, Consulting, and Demo Delivery
During discovery, you find the VP of Engineering enthusiastic and vocal about the solution, but the actual purchase decision requires sign-off from a CFO who has never attended a single meeting or been named in any conversation. This gap represents a failure to identify which stakeholder role?
The economic buyer holds final budget authority and must be identified and engaged early in stakeholder mapping; failing to surface this person — even when a champion is enthusiastic — is a classic discovery gap that stalls deals late in the cycle.
Question 3 of 6 · Client Engagement, Consulting, and Demo Delivery
Which of the following is the MOST common reason a technically successful PoC fails to secure budget approval to advance into an MVP?
The PoC-to-MVP transition depends on a defensible business case; if results are presented as pure technical proof without tying outcomes back to the ROI metrics agreed during scoping, budget holders have no basis to approve continued investment.
Question 4 of 6 · Client Engagement, Consulting, and Demo Delivery
During a solution scoping workshop, you need to establish quantifiable success metrics for a document-processing AI agent PoC before build begins. Which combination of metrics BEST anchors the eventual ROI conversation with the business sponsor?
Anchoring success metrics to a documented baseline cost/time and a projected dollar-value reduction gives the business sponsor a direct ROI calculation, which is the currency executives use to approve continued investment.
Question 5 of 6 · Client Engagement, Consulting, and Demo Delivery
In the Problem-Insight-Solution-Impact demo narrative arc, what is the primary purpose of the 'Insight' stage that sits between Problem and Solution?
The Insight stage exists to surface a surprising, client-specific finding that reframes the stated problem as more urgent or differently-shaped than the client assumed, which is what makes the subsequent Solution feel earned and necessary rather than generic.
Question 6 of 6 · Client Engagement, Consulting, and Demo Delivery
Two weeks after a production AI agent went live, it begins producing incorrect outputs that affect the client's downstream billing system. The client's ops team escalates urgently. As the FDE acting in a trusted-advisor role, what is the BEST first step per crisis management protocol?
Crisis management protocol prioritizes rapid, transparent communication and visible mitigation ahead of full diagnosis — this preserves trust during a high-stakes incident (billing impact) and demonstrates the trusted-advisor posture the client expects from an FDE.
