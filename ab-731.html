Question 1 of 12 · Business value of generative AI
A retail company's HR department wants to use generative AI to draft job descriptions, while finance wants to use it to forecast quarterly revenue variance. A transformation leader is asked to prioritize which use case to pilot first. Which factor should carry the MOST weight in this prioritization decision?
High-value use case prioritization frameworks weigh impact against feasibility (data availability, risk, and organizational readiness for change) — not novelty of technology or historical budget allocation.
Question 2 of 12 · Microsoft AI apps and services
An HR department wants to let employees ask natural-language questions about internal HR policies (leave, benefits, expense rules) stored in SharePoint and get a conversational answer inside Microsoft Teams. HR has no developers and needs a low-code way to build and publish this experience quickly. Which Microsoft AI capability BEST meets this requirement?
Copilot Studio is the low-code platform purpose-built for business users to author, ground, and publish a custom conversational agent (e.g., to Teams) without writing code.
Question 3 of 12 · Implementation and adoption
A retail company completed a Microsoft 365 Copilot pilot with 50 users in finance. Usage data shows only 20% of pilot users opened Copilot more than twice in the first month, and manager feedback indicates employees don't understand which tasks Copilot can help with. The AI Transformation Leader must decide the next step before scaling to 500 users. Which action BEST addresses the root cause before expanding the pilot?
Low usage combined with lack of task awareness points to a skilling and use-case gap, not a licensing or scale problem; targeted, role-based enablement tied to real workflows is the standard remediation before scaling a low-performing pilot.
Question 4 of 12 · Business value of generative AI
A CFO asks the AI transformation leader to justify continued investment in a Copilot rollout after six months. Which approach BEST demonstrates business value to build the ongoing business case?
A durable business case requires tying deployment to measurable outcomes and KPIs defined at the outset — quantifying time savings, adoption, and impact on business metrics — not vanity metrics like license counts or feature counts.

The Copilot Dashboard, delivered through Viva Insights, is purpose-built to give leaders ongoing visibility into Copilot usage trends, adoption by group, and estimated impact, which is exactly what an executive steering committee needs for adoption reporting.

Question 8 of 12 · Microsoft AI apps and services
Which underlying mechanism does Microsoft 365 Copilot rely on to ground its responses in a specific organization's emails, files, chats, and calendar data while respecting each user's existing permissions?
Microsoft Graph connects Copilot to organizational content across Microsoft 365 (mail, files, chat, calendar) in a permission-aware way, which is how Copilot grounds responses in tenant-specific data.
Question 9 of 12 · Implementation and adoption
A company's Copilot Center of Excellence wants to prevent Microsoft 365 Copilot from surfacing content from an HR SharePoint site labeled 'Confidential - HR Only' in responses to employees outside HR. Which configuration should the governance team implement to enforce this?
Copilot respects existing Microsoft 365 permissions, sensitivity labels, and Purview DLP policies at query time, so properly scoping labels and DLP to the HR site is the correct governance control to prevent unauthorized content surfacing.
Question 10 of 12 · Business value of generative AI

Responsible AI at the organizational level requires leadership to own governance, risk classification, accountability, and transparency practices in response to regulatory pressure such as the EU AI Act — this is a leadership concern, not solely legal or technical.
