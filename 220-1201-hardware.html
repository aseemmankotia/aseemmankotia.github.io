Question 1 of 6 · Hardware
A file server hosts a database that requires maximum read/write performance while also tolerating a single drive failure without downtime. The company has budgeted for exactly 4 identical drives. Which RAID level BEST meets these requirements?
RAID 10 stripes data across two mirrored pairs, delivering the highest read/write performance of any redundant array while tolerating the loss of one drive per mirrored pair. With exactly 4 drives, it fully uses the budget for both speed and fault tolerance.
Question 2 of 6 · Hardware
A technician is installing a new NVMe SSD that uses the PCIe x4 interface into a laptop's M.2 slot. Which M.2 key type must the drive's edge connector use for it to run at full PCIe x4 speed?
An M-key-only M.2 edge connector exposes all four PCIe lanes, allowing NVMe SSDs to run at full PCIe x4 speed. This is the standard keying for high-performance NVMe storage.
Question 3 of 6 · Hardware
During the laser printing imaging process, a corona wire or roller applies a uniform negative charge across the surface of the photosensitive drum before the laser writes the image. Which step of the process does this describe?
The charging step applies a uniform negative charge to the drum's surface using a primary corona wire or charging roller, preparing it for the laser to selectively discharge areas during exposing.
Question 4 of 6 · Hardware
Which statement BEST describes the primary benefit of ECC (Error Correcting Code) RAM compared to standard non-ECC RAM?
ECC RAM uses extra bits to detect and correct single-bit errors in memory, improving data integrity and stability. This makes it essential for servers and workstations running critical applications where silent data corruption is unacceptable.
Question 5 of 6 · Hardware
A technician is building a workstation with two high-end GPUs, each requiring two 8-pin PCIe power connectors, plus a CPU requiring an 8-pin EPS connector. The available 650W PSU has one 8-pin EPS connector, two 6-pin PCIe connectors, and two 6+2 pin PCIe connectors. What is the BEST course of action?
Dual high-end GPUs plus a CPU typically exceed 650W of combined draw, and relying on adapters for high-current PCIe connections is unreliable and unsafe. The correct fix is a PSU upgrade with sufficient wattage headroom and enough native 8-pin PCIe connectors to match each GPU's requirements exactly.
Question 6 of 6 · Hardware
A designer needs to connect three monitors to a laptop that has only one video output port, without adding external graphics hardware, and wants to daisy-chain the displays together using a single cable run between them. Which connector technology is required for this daisy-chain configuration?
DisplayPort's Multi-Stream Transport (MST) feature allows a single DisplayPort output to drive multiple monitors daisy-chained together, splitting the video stream between displays that support MST pass-through.
