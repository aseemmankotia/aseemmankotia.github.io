Question 1 of 6 · Identify Business Needs and Solutions
A retail bank wants to identify unusual credit card transactions that deviate from an individual cardholder's historical spending behavior in real time, flagging them for fraud review. Which of the Seven Patterns of AI BEST matches this business need?
The need is to detect deviation from a known individual baseline (anomaly detection against an established pattern), which is precisely what the Patterns and Anomalies pattern addresses.
Question 2 of 6 · Identify Business Needs and Solutions
During Business Understanding, a CPMAI-certified project manager evaluates automating invoice approval for a finance department that processes 40 invoices per week under three stable, well-documented approval rules that rarely change. Which feasibility conclusion is MOST appropriate?
CPMAI feasibility evaluation requires honestly comparing AI against traditional alternatives; low volume, deterministic and stable rules with no ambiguity mean a rule-based/RPA solution is cheaper, more explainable, and equally effective.
Question 3 of 6 · Identify Business Needs and Solutions
An HR team wants to deploy an AI tool to automatically screen and rank job applicant resumes before a recruiter reviews them. During the risk assessment, which risk category should be given the HIGHEST priority for this specific use case?
CPMAI's risk assessment separates security, safety, and ethics; the dominant risk for resume screening is biased or discriminatory decision-making learned from historical hiring data, which is fundamentally an ethics concern with legal and reputational consequences.
Question 4 of 6 · Identify Business Needs and Solutions
Midway through Business Understanding, after the AI project scope statement has been drafted and approved, the executive sponsor requests adding real-time streaming inference capability that was not part of the original scope, and the data governance team confirms the required real-time data pipeline does not yet exist. According to CPMAI scope discipline, what should the project manager do?
CPMAI treats the scope statement as a control document; a new capability that introduces a new feasibility constraint (a missing prerequisite pipeline) requires formal change control and re-evaluation against go/no-go criteria, not silent absorption or blind execution.
Question 5 of 6 · Identify Business Needs and Solutions
An e-commerce company wants each customer to see a uniquely tailored homepage layout, product bundle, and pricing offer generated specifically from that individual's own behavior, rather than a segment-level recommendation shared across similar customers. Which of the Seven Patterns of AI does this use case represent?
Hyperpersonalization Patterns tailor experiences, content, and offers to a specific individual using their unique behavioral data, which is distinct from segment-based predictive modeling.
Question 6 of 6 · Identify Business Needs and Solutions
When building the total cost of ownership (TCO) estimate for an AI initiative during Business Understanding, which cost component is MOST frequently underestimated or omitted entirely by organizations new to AI projects?
Unlike traditional software, AI models degrade over time due to data and concept drift and require continuous labeling, monitoring, and retraining; these recurring operational costs are the most commonly underestimated in TCO because teams tend to focus only on one-time build costs.
